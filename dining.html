"AADAB" MULTI-CUISINE RESTAURANT & BAR

Adab a Multi Cuisine Restaurant, with seating capacity of sixty covers in a table combination of four each make a comfortable dining for the Family, Couples and small gatherings. As the name suggest, the cozy interiors of the Restaurant reflect the inherited culture of Lucknow. Well located in plus green serenity of Gomti nagar, the Restaurant offers the best of Indian, Chinese and Continental delicacies. Our well trained staff makes dining experience a memorable one. Chefs from different corner of the country serve you the best of delicacies cooked to perfection taking care of Vegetarian and Non Vegetarian preparations separately. Adab is the name to welcome you all to the world of finest selection of dishes and best of services to make your every visit a memorable one.

"JANNAT" ROOF-TOP RESTAURANT & BAR

High above the city, the Rooftop Outdoor Restaurant & Bar embraces the fresh Lucknow breeze, while offering a magnificent view of the City. There can hardly be a better milieu anywhere in the world for delectable food, pleasant music and casual al fresco dining. With a large and varied menu for both lunch and dinner, Jannat features fresh natural ingredients infused in simple cooking; from grilled meat and fish dishes, fresh salads and pastas, to a selection of mouth-watering desserts. Jannat offers something to satisfy every taste, and an atmosphere of casual intimacy for every occasion. Whether you are celebrating your stay in the magical city , or looking to unwind with a drink after a busy day, the Jannat restro bar is a place to remember and experience time and time again.

Lobby Level Experience

"Aadab" Multi-Cuisine Restaurant & Bar

Aadab is a sixty-cover fine dining haven located at the lobby level, styled in warm amber tones reflecting Lucknow's inherited royal Tehzeeb. Designed for families, couples, and intimate gatherings, it features four-seater comfortable seating arrangements with ambient lighting.

Our master chefs present the finest authentic Indian, royal Awadhi dum pukht delicacies, sizzlers, Chinese, and Continental menus with separate dedicated preparation kitchens for vegetarian and non-vegetarian fare.

Open-Air Rooftop

"Jannat" Rooftop Restaurant & Bar

High above the city, Jannat embraces the refreshing Lucknow breeze while offering an unobstructed, mesmerizing panoramic view of Gomti Nagar. It is widely hailed as one of the most romantic spots in Lucknow for delectable food, pleasant acoustic music, and casual al fresco dining under starlit skies.

From charcoal-grilled kebabs, wood-fired pizzas, fresh pastas, to artisanal hand-crafted cocktails and mocktails, Jannat ensures every evening is truly unforgettable.

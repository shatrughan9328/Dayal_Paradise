Welcome To Lucknow's Finest

Comfort, Serenity & Luxury in Vipul Khand

Dayal Paradise – a plush address of Comfort, Serenity, Luxury & Greenery, is located in the tranquil backdrops of Vipul Khand, Gomti Nagar. Amidst multiplexes, recreational parks, and corporate & business houses, it is seamlessly connected to the Railway Station, City Bus Terminal, Airport, and Amar Shaheed Path.

An ideal abode for vacations, conferences, weddings, or celebratory galas, it is also Lucknow’s preferred family-friendly hotel, complete with 55 designer rooms, award-winning multi-cuisine dining, and expansive banqueting halls.
